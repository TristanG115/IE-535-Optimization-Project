## Required end-of-session closeout

At the end of every session, when completing authorized work:

1. Update the AI documentation. Maintain one current raw export, one transcript and one tracker row per tool/session ID. Refresh the entire actual saved conversation, including prior turns, follow-ups and final replies, in place when that session continues. Replace superseded snapshots only after verifying the new Codex source extends the prior source; retain earlier revisions in Git. Never invent or reconstruct missing content.
2. Record important recommendations, corrections and decisions actually discussed in `ai_record/decisions.md`, including reasons and observed verification. Leave decisions requiring the user's approval explicitly marked as pending.
3. When a local commit is authorized, include the final reply by starting `records.py codex <actual-source> --topic <topic> --wait-final --commit` in a hidden background process before sending it (see ai_record/README.md). If the user requests review before committing, do not start an auto-commit watcher; present a snapshot and the generated artifact for review first. After approval, refresh the saved conversation and commit. Never label unsaved or missing content complete, concatenate overlapping snapshots, or fabricate a final reply.
4. Verify the completed changes, then commit them and the updated AI records at each major checkpoint and at completion if changes remain. Push the resulting commits to the configured remote, including any final-reply record commit once its capture succeeds. Honor any current review-before-commit/push request first. Report actual commit/push results and any pending final-reply capture; a running watcher does not establish a successful commit or push.
5. Give a short final summary of what was accomplished, any concerns or pending approvals, and the recommended next step. State any remaining export gap honestly.
